# Zeta Prop — Product Overview

Zeta Prop is a SaaS CRM platform for Argentine real estate agencies. It centralizes property management, rental administration, client tracking, and financial operations into a single system, enhanced with AI capabilities.

## Core Modules

- **Properties**: Full CRUD for listings with images, geolocation, and branch assignment
- **Rentals (Alquileres)**: Lease contracts, payment periods, inflation adjustments (IPC/ICL), late fees, and auto-generated Word contracts
- **CRM & Leads**: Kanban pipeline (Nuevo → Contactado → Visita → Reservado) with AI-assisted lead classification
- **Tenant Portal**: Passwordless access via rental code + DNI; shows payment history and upcoming due dates
- **Owner Portal**: Liquidations and property summaries for landlords
- **Multi-branch**: Properties, agents, and reports scoped per branch
- **White-label Sites**: Each agency gets a public-facing website at their own domain
- **AI Valuation (Tasación)**: ML-based property price estimation

## Target Users

- Real estate agency admins and managers
- Agents (limited scope per branch)
- Tenants (read-only portal)
- Property owners (liquidation reports)

## Market

Argentina — content and UI are in Spanish (es-AR). Currency handling covers both ARS and USD.
